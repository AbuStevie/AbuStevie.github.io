Computational Thinking Among Electronic Music Producers

This questionnaire is intended for individuals with a background in electronic music production (using DAW software) and aims to measure your attitudes and skills regarding complex problem-solving.

  • The questionnaire is completely anonymous and collects no identifying information.
  • Those interested in participating in the coupon raffle can leave their details at the end of the questionnaire.
  • Estimated completion time is approximately 30 minutes.
  • The questionnaire is optimized for mobile phones and tablets.

This questionnaire is administered as part of a study conducted at Tel Aviv University's School of Education, supervised by Prof. Arnon Hershkovitz. Completing the questionnaire constitutes consent to participate in the study.

For further information, please contact Ofir Vander at ofirvander@mail.tau.ac.il
